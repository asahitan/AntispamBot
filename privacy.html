Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 28, 2024 · Last updated: November 28, 2024

This policy covers what AntiSpam collects when it's running in your server, why, and how to request access or deletion.

1. Introduction

AntiSpam ("the Bot") is committed to protecting your privacy and the security of the data it processes. This policy outlines what data the Bot collects, how it's used, and your rights regarding that information. By using the Bot, you agree to the terms below.

2. Data we collect

2.1 User data

  • User IDs — used to identify and manage user activity on Discord servers.
  • Messages — messages flagged as spam or as violating server rules may be temporarily stored for analysis and action.

2.2 Server data

  • Server IDs — used to store server-specific settings and preferences.
  • Configuration settings — anti-spam rules and preferences set by server administrators.

2.3 Interaction data

  • Logs of interactions with the Bot, such as command usage, to improve performance and troubleshoot issues.

3. How we use collected data

Collected data is used to:

  • Detect and mitigate spam or abusive behavior on Discord servers.
  • Improve the functionality and performance of the Bot.
  • Provide troubleshooting and support to users and server administrators.

The Bot does not use collected data for marketing or advertising purposes.

4. Data sharing

The Bot does not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties. Data may only be shared:

  • When required by law or legal proceedings.
  • With Discord, in compliance with their Terms of Service and Developer Policies.

5. Data retention

  • Temporary data — messages flagged as spam are stored temporarily and deleted within 30 days unless required for ongoing investigations.
  • Server settings — stored for as long as the Bot is active on the server, or until removed by the server administrator.

6. Security

We prioritize the security of your data by implementing appropriate technical and organizational measures. However, no system can guarantee complete security — you're responsible for the security of your own Discord account and server.

7. Your rights

As a user, you have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access — request access to the data associated with your account or server.
  • Deletion — request removal of your data from the Bot's systems.
  • Correction — request updates to incorrect or outdated information.

To exercise your rights, contact us using the details below.

8. Children's privacy

The Bot is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 13. If you believe data has been collected from a child under 13 without consent, please contact us and we will delete the data promptly.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Significant changes will be communicated via email or our Discord support server. Continued use of the Bot after updates implies acceptance of the revised policy.
